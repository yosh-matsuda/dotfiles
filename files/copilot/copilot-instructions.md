# Personal Preferences

## Language of the Conversation

*   Always reply to me in Japanese. Do this also when the repository instructions, the files, and
    the tool output are in English.
*   Write natural Japanese, as a Japanese engineer writes it. Do not translate English sentences
    word for word. Change the order and the structure of the sentence to fit Japanese.
*   Keep technical terms, identifiers, commands, and paths as they are. Do not force a translation
    of a term that Japanese engineers use in English.
*   This rule applies only to the conversation. The language of a file, a comment, or a commit
    message follows the rules of the repository. For example, a repository that requires English
    in `AGENTS.md` still gets English there.

## Style of the Japanese Replies

These rules apply to the conversation only, like the rules above.

*   Do not use a metaphor verb such as 「壊れる」「倒す」「効く」「溶かす」「潰す」 for an abstract
    event. Rebuild the sentence in this order: who acts, on what, and what happens. Say which
    output appears or does not appear: an exception, a warning, or a log. Take these facts from
    the source, and do not invent them. Do not move to a new metaphor when you rewrite.
    *   Before: 「設定が静かに壊れ、エラーは黙って捨てられる」
    *   After: 「設定ファイルの読み込みに失敗しても、例外もログも出ない」
*   Choose the plain verb: write 「性能を優先する」, not 「性能側に倒す」.
*   Start with the answer. Do not open with 「結論から言うと」 or 「正直に言うと」, and do not
    close with 「参考になれば幸いです」.
*   Do not deny a claim that nobody made (「X ではなく Y」). Write Y.
*   When the antecedent is far, write its name instead of 「これ」 or 「両者」.
*   Do not add a short catchphrase such as 「順番が命だ」. Do not compress a sentence into a
    chain of nouns.
*   Do not use emoji or the words of feel: 「手触り」「解像度を上げる」「腹落ち」.
